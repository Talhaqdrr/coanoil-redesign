Time to upgrade? Watch for these signs.

Thinking about replacing your boiler, burner, furnace, water heater or oil tank? Our technicians will evaluate your system, right-size a new one, point you to rebates, and help you secure zero-interest financing for tank and tank line replacements.

  • The system requires multiple service calls per year
  • The boiler or furnace is more than 15 years old
  • Some rooms are much hotter or colder than others
  • Heating costs keep rising even when you use the system less
  • Your system’s highest potential AFUE is lower than 80 percent

An annual tune-up alone can improve heating efficiency by up to 10 percent.

No ductwork? No problem.

Traditional ductwork is no longer required for central air. We install systems that use “mini-ducts” that fit inside existing walls, with virtually no alterations to your home.

Upgrading from a 10-SEER unit to a modern 16-SEER system generally yields an efficiency improvement of up to 40 percent — an excellent return and a quick payback.

    Price protection & payment plans

    Lock out inflation. Smooth out winter bills.

    Energy prices are volatile. Protect the price of all or part of your annual supply, and your price is protected until all protected gallons are delivered. Contracts affect only your fuel price and last no longer than one year.

    Capped Price

    A maximum price per gallon with the opportunity for a lower price if the market moves down — you pay the lower of the cap or our daily posted price on the day of delivery.

    ProsProtected if prices rise; you get the lower price if they fall.
    ConsAn affordable, reasonable fee based on gallons protected.

    Pre-Buy Price

    Lock in a set price for a specific number of gallons with one full payment. Optional downside protection gives you the posted price or your locked-in price, whichever is lower.

    ProsInflation risk eliminated; one payment up front, none during the season.
    ConsWithout downside protection, no benefit if prices decrease.

    Quality, reliability and integrity since 1946.

    Francis Coan founded the company in 1946 after returning home from World War II. He started with a small gasoline station on Lincoln Square in Natick, where he was known for washing the customer’s windshield both inside and out — exceeding expectations and giving extra value for the money.

    Soon after, Fran entered the home heating oil business. Today his son Kevin is President, and we remain a family-owned business with customer service as our hallmark. We’ve never forgotten that the success of the business rests with that “Most Important Person” — the Coan customer.

    Conservation tips

    We’re here to help you save energy.

    Our conservation expertise is free. Request a consultation any time — or start saving today with these tips.

    Cold weather

    • Correct air leaks around windows, doors and outlets
    • Repair weather stripping and caulking
    • Make sure your ceiling has at least six inches of insulation
    • Wrap pipes to guard against heat loss and freezing
    • Get a heating tune-up before the season begins
    • Close the kitchen vent, fireplace damper and closet doors

    Warm weather

    • Keep A/C coils free of dust and dirt
    • Change or clean your A/C filter monthly
    • Look for ENERGY STAR® approved units
    • Cook on the grill to keep heat outside
    • Close windows and curtains against daytime sun
    • Don’t open windows when it’s warmer outside than in

    All year long

    • A programmable thermostat can shave $100 off annual bills
    • Choose a high-efficiency indirect water heater
    • Keep the oven door closed — it can drop 25 degrees
    • Wash clothes in the coolest water possible
    • A five-minute shower uses about 7.5 gallons vs. up to 20 for a bath
    • Dry loads back-to-back
